# TurmiPatch AI Concept Note

TurmiPatch AI is an early-stage health-tech concept focused on AI-powered wound care support.

The idea combines turmeric-inspired natural healing science with intelligent recovery monitoring. The future vision is to create a smart wound healing patch that can observe healing progress, detect potential risks, and provide useful AI-driven insights.

## Problem

Wound care can be slow, expensive, and difficult to monitor. Some wounds heal normally, while others may become infected or take longer than expected to recover.

Patients and healthcare providers may benefit from earlier visibility into healing progress and risk factors.

## Proposed Solution

TurmiPatch AI aims to explore a smart wound care approach that could combine:

- A turmeric-inspired healing patch concept
- AI-based wound recovery tracking
- Early risk detection
- Data-driven recovery insights
- Future integration with smart sensors or mobile applications

## Long-Term Aim

The long-term aim is to contribute to preventative, accessible, and scalable wound-care innovation in the UK and globally.

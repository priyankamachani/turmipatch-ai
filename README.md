# TurmiPatch AI

TurmiPatch AI is an early-stage health-tech project exploring AI-powered wound care innovation inspired by turmeric and enhanced by intelligent recovery monitoring.

The concept aims to combine natural healing science, smart patch technology, and artificial intelligence to support faster, safer, and more personalised wound recovery.

## Vision

TurmiPatch is designed as a future smart wound healing patch that could help monitor wound recovery patterns, identify early signs of infection or delayed healing, and provide AI-driven recovery insights.

The long-term goal is to contribute to scalable wound-care innovation in the UK and globally.

## Why This Matters

Wound care is a major healthcare challenge. Delayed healing, infection risks, chronic wounds, and repeated clinical visits place pressure on patients, clinicians, and healthcare systems.

TurmiPatch AI explores how artificial intelligence and smart monitoring could help improve wound-care outcomes.

## Core Concept

- AI-assisted wound healing monitoring
- Recovery progress tracking
- Early detection of delayed healing risks
- Turmeric-inspired natural healing approach
- Smart patch and wearable health technology
- Future clinical and healthcare integration

## Project Status

This project is currently in the concept and research stage.

The TurmiPatch domain and LinkedIn presence were created in August 2025. Due to not being able to open a limited company restrictions, the project has not yet been launched as an active startup. The current focus is on research, documentation, concept development, and future AI prototype planning.

## Future Development

1. Research wound-care challenges and healthcare impact
2. Review turmeric and curcumin wound healing literature
3. Design the AI monitoring concept
4. Create sample wound recovery datasets
5. Build an early AI prototype for healing progress prediction
6. Explore ethical, clinical, and regulatory considerations

## Disclaimer

TurmiPatch AI is an early-stage research and innovation concept. It is not a medical device, treatment, or clinical product. Any future development would require scientific validation, clinical testing, regulatory review, and appropriate medical supervision.
